ABOUT US

Established in 1990, Muay Thai Academy International is one of the United States' oldest active Muay Thai academies.

We specialize in offensive and defensive tactics. Serving civilians, law enforcement, military personnel, and high-risk operations specialists.

OUR PROGRAM

Education

We specialize in survival-focused combat tactics, emphasizing military and old style Muay Thai.

Training

Supervised training sessions and personalized programs allow students to progress at their own pace, enjoying unlimited training during flexible hours and accessing workshops at no extra cost

Development

Successful students at our academy progress through personal training logs and evaluations, with opportunities to advance to CQCT training, typically reserved for law enforcement and military personel
